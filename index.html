WHAT IS E-CELL?

Entrepreneurship Cell of Datta Meghe College of Engineering is a student-driven community dedicated to promoting innovation, creativity and the spirit of entrepreneurship.

It serves as a platform where students can learn, network and turn their ideas into meaningful ventures through workshops, competitions, mentorship and industry interactions.
